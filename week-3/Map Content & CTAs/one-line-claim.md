## Why I Chose This Claim

I chose this claim because it focuses on what I actually build rather than simply listing technologies.

It allows my portfolio to demonstrate my skills through projects such as WellBot and my AI-related internship experience.

The claim also leaves room for different types of AI and software projects instead of limiting my portfolio to one specific technology or industry.

Most importantly, the claim can be proven through evidence such as:

- Working projects
- GitHub repositories
- Live demos
- Screenshots
- Technical architecture
- Internship experience
- Project results
