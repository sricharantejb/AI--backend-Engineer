# My Four Constraints

## 1. Budget

The portfolio must be completely free to build and host.

I should not need to pay for:

- Hosting
- Domain during the initial build
- Database
- Backend infrastructure
- Premium templates
- Paid development tools

A free subdomain is acceptable for the first version.
